## Prompt 3: Growth

**Prompt:**
Suggest 3 low-cost AI tools a bakery in lahore can use
to attract university students with a budget of 15000 PKR per month.

**AI answer:**
### 1. Canva (Magic Studio)
**Purpose:** Visual Content & Graphic Design.
University students eat with their eyes first. Canva’s new AI "Magic Studio" allows you to create 
professional-looking Instagram stories and posts without a designer.
*   **How to use it for students:** Use the **AI Image Generator** to create dreamy backgrounds for 
your cakes, or the **Magic Edit** tool to change the color of a frosting in a photo to match a 
university's theme (e.g., making a cake "LUMS Blue" or "FAST Green" for a special promo).
*   **Estimated Cost:** Free version is great, but **Canva Pro** is roughly **1,500 – 2,000 
PKR/month**.

### 2. ChatGPT (Free Version or Plus)
**Purpose:** Copywriting, Promo Planning, and Menu Engineering.
Writing "corporate" captions doesn't work with Gen Z. You need a voice that is witty, relatable, and 
uses a mix of English and Roman Urdu.
*   **How to use it for students:** Ask ChatGPT to: *"Write 5 witty Instagram captions for a 'Late 
Night Study Session' brownie box for university students in Lahore. Use a mix of English and Roman 
Urdu and include Gen Z slang."* It can also help you brainstorm "Exam Season" bundles or "Break-up 
Cake" promos.
*   **Estimated Cost:** **Free** (The free version is more than enough for a small bakery).

### 3. CapCut (AI Video Editor)
**Purpose:** Viral TikToks and Instagram Reels.
Students rarely look at static photos; they watch short-form video. CapCut (owned by ByteDance) has 
powerful AI features that make editing effortless.
*   **How to use it for students:** Use the **"Auto-Caption"** feature (crucial because many students 
watch videos on mute in class) and **AI-powered templates** to sync your cake-cutting videos to 
trending sounds. A 7-second "satisfying" video of chocolate pouring over a cake can go viral locally.
*   **Estimated Cost:** **Free** (Pro version is optional and very affordable).

---

### 💰 Budget Allocation Strategy (15,000 PKR)

Since the tools above are very cheap, **do not spend your whole budget on software.** AI creates the 
content, but you need to pay to get that content in front of the students.

| Item | Estimated Monthly Cost | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Canva Pro** | ~2,000 PKR | Professional visuals & branding |
| **ChatGPT / CapCut** | 0 PKR | Content & Video editing |
| **Meta Ads (Instagram/FB)** | 10,000 PKR | Targeted ads (Pin-drop location around LUMS, FAST, UCP, 
ITU, etc.) |
| **Micro-Influencer Treats** | 3,000 PKR | Cost of free samples sent to 2-3 student vloggers per 
month |
| **TOTAL** | **15,000 PKR** | |

**Manager's Tip:** To really attract students in Lahore, run a **"Student ID Discount"** (e.g., 10% 
off with a valid Uni ID). Use your AI tools to announce this specifically on Instagram Stories between 
4:00 PM and 7:00 PM, which is when students are usually craving a snack!
